# Advertising, Sponsorship & Partnership Policy

**Project:** Free TV & Radio — Online Lounge  
**Record date:** September 29, 2026  
**Status:** Planning / policy record; advertising is not being represented as active unless separately implemented and verified.

## Our basic idea

Free TV & Radio is intended to feel like a comfortable online coffee-shop lounge: people can watch TV, listen to radio, read, listen to audiobooks, and relax.

Our advertising philosophy is intentionally restrained. Advertising must support the free service without taking over the experience.

## Advertising limits

1. **No more than two advertisements per viewer per hour** as the planned maximum.
2. **Target advertisement length: approximately 10–15 seconds**, with 15 seconds as the upper target for a normal ad.
3. Ads should be **discreet and low-volume or muted** where practical.
4. Ads must **never cover, obscure, or replace the TV/video player**.
5. Ads should **not interrupt active viewing, reading, or listening**.
6. No forced full-screen interruptions.
7. No deceptive pop-ups, fake system warnings, or misleading controls.
8. No advertising design that makes normal site navigation difficult.
9. Advertising should feel more like a quiet notice in a coffee shop than a loud commercial break.
10. Accessibility and a calm user experience take priority over maximizing ad impressions.

## Partnership opportunities

We may approach companies such as Amazon/Kindle and other legitimate organizations about:

- Appropriate advertising or sponsorship programs.
- Infrastructure or cloud/storage assistance.
- Grants or technology credits.
- Financial sponsorship.
- Partnerships supporting free books, audiobooks, reading, listening, TV, radio, or other lounge features.

Any partnership would be subject to the company's own terms and to our site's safety, privacy, copyright, and user-experience requirements.

We are **not** asking partners to provide us with copyrighted books or other content for unauthorized redistribution. Reading and audiobook resources should remain properly licensed, public-domain, or otherwise legitimately available.

## How support would be used

If advertising or sponsorship revenue becomes available, the intended purpose is to help support legitimate project expenses such as:

- Hosting and cloud infrastructure.
- Storage and bandwidth.
- Development and maintenance.
- Security and reliability.
- Accessibility and user-experience improvements.
- Future free features for visitors.

Revenue should eventually be handled through an appropriate dedicated business/payment structure rather than casually mixing project funds with a personal payment account.

## The coffee-shop principle

The site should remain welcoming and calm.

**Our rule of thumb:** If an advertisement would feel so disruptive that a quiet coffee shop would never tolerate the equivalent behavior, it does not belong in our lounge.

We want visitors to remember the TV show, radio station, book, audiobook, or conversation—not the advertisement.

## Future brochures and outreach

This policy is also a source document for future:

## Important distinction

This document records our **planned policy and goals**. It does not itself create an advertising relationship, guarantee sponsorship, or guarantee that any company will provide money, storage, cloud credits, or other support.
