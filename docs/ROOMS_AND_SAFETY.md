# Rooms & Safety

## Purpose

Rooms are intended to provide simple public or private spaces for voice and video conversation while giving hosts and participants understandable controls.

## Room types

- **Public rooms:** discoverable/joinable according to the site's final access rules.
- **Private rooms:** access controlled by invitation or other explicit permission.

## Voice and video

Most Rooms may use voice or video. The interface should make microphone and camera state obvious so people know when they are speaking or sharing video.

## Participant controls

Participants should have clear access to:

- Mute themselves
- Turn their camera on or off
- Block another participant
- Report a concern

## Host controls

Room hosts should have appropriate controls to:

- Mute a participant
- Remove a participant
- Restrict participation
- Ban a participant from the room

Platform administration remains separate from ordinary room-host authority.

## Safety principles

The project is designed to address harassment, bullying, sexual harassment, threats, exploitation, and other abusive conduct.

A report should be treated seriously, but a report by itself should not automatically be treated as proof. Moderation should consider context and available evidence.

The system should also account for:

- Bad-faith or knowingly false reports
- Appeals
- Evidence preservation
- Urgent protective action for credible serious threats
- Stronger safeguards for minors
- Privacy and clear reporting paths

## Before broad launch

Rooms should receive a final community-rules, legal, privacy, and safety review before broad public launch. The public documentation should be updated when the actual implementation differs from this plan.
